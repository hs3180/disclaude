## Responsibility boundary

Agents and external skills choose uses, providers, endpoints, credential exchanges and credential lifecycle. Disclaude provides an independent handoff path and enforces its identity, destination and lifecycle boundaries. It does not classify ordinary content, interpret provider scopes, or add global logger/CLI/harness stderr filtering for this work.

#4973 tracks the generic delegation contract and its open audience/transport questions. #4974 is explicitly part of 0.5.2: move GitHub-specific skills out and provide a quick way to build external skills. Neither authorizes a general IAM or security-policy engine. Other original P1 work remains outside this release.
